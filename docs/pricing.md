# Alpinist Studios — Pricing Guide

Document Version: 1.0
Document Classification: Public
Last Updated: July 2026

Alpinist Studios prices engagements based on scope, team composition, delivery model,
and the technology complexity involved. The pricing models below cover the company's
full service portfolio. All prices are listed in USD and are indicative; final quotes are
provided after a discovery and scoping assessment.

---

## 1. Engagement & Pricing Models

Alpinist Studios offers four primary pricing models so clients can choose the approach
that best matches their budget, risk tolerance, and delivery needs.

| Model | Best for | Billing basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fixed-Scope | Well-defined projects with stable requirements | Fixed price per milestone |
| Time & Materials (T&M) | Evolving scope and fast iteration | Hourly / weekly rate |
| Dedicated Team | Long-term product development | Monthly team retainer |
| Staff Augmentation | Filling specific skill gaps in an existing team | Monthly per-engineer rate |

A 10% discount applies to annual contracts committed under the Dedicated Team model.
A 15% discount applies to new clients who sign an engagement of three months or longer.

## 8. Payment Terms

- A 40% deposit is required to schedule work, followed by milestone-based payments.
- For Time & Materials engagements, invoices are issued bi-weekly based on hours logged.
- For Dedicated Team and Staff Augmentation, invoices are issued monthly in advance.
- Payment is due within 30 days of the invoice date.
- Fixed-scope projects use a milestone schedule (typically 40% start, 40% mid-point,
  20% on delivery), which may be customized per project.

## 9. Additional Notes

- Pricing excludes taxes, third-party software licensing, cloud infrastructure spend, and
  travel unless otherwise agreed.
- Prices may vary based on seniority of the assigned team, geographic location, and
  integration complexity.
- A final, binding quote is issued after the discovery and assessment phase.
- Support beyond the included post-launch window is available under a separate
  maintenance or support agreement (SLA).
